=== item:siftite_helmet | Siftite Helmet
Siftite armor (durability 800, armor 2/7/5/2, toughness 1). **With the full set**, spirit liquid only drains your health to 50 % instead of 20 %. See [[page:soul|Soul Deprivation]].

=== item:siftite_chestplate | Siftite Chestplate
The siftite chestplate. The full set keeps 50 % of your health against soul deprivation. A [[pysift:sift_beacon_gem|Sift Beacon Gem]] can be inlaid into it at a smithing table.

=== item:siftite_sword | Siftite Sword
Siftite tools: durability 800, mining speed 7, attack +2.5. Made from [[pysift:siftite_ingot|siftite ingots]].

=== item:aurum_pickaxe | Aurum Pickaxe
Aurum tools: durability 450, mining speed 10, enchantability 22, mine iron-tier blocks. Fast and easy to enchant, but they wear out sooner.

=== item:soulite_sword | Soulite Sword
Soulite gear (durability 2600, mining speed 10, attack +5; armor 4/9/7/4, toughness 4). Upgrade at a smithing table with a [[pysift:soulite_upgrade_smithing_template|Soulite Upgrade Template]], netherite gear and a [[pysift:soulite_ingot|soulite ingot]].

Every piece has its own power:
| Piece | Power |
|---|---|
| Sword | Every 3rd hit chains to 2 targets |
| Axe | A fully charged attack grows exploding sculk maws |
| Pickaxe, shovel, hoe | 15 % chance of [[pysift:soul_essence|soul essence]] from ores |
| Helmet | Enemies within 16 blocks glow |
| Chestplate | Below 30 % health, leaves a decoy and gives Speed II (60 s cooldown) |
| Leggings | Less knockback; attackers take 3 damage from sculk thorns |
| Boots | No fall damage; walk on spirit liquid |
| Full set | Immune to soul deprivation |

=== item:soulite_ingot | Soulite Ingot
The final material. Shapeless: netherite ingot, [[pysift:resonant_crystal|resonant crystal]], [[pysift:monarch_feather|monarch feather]], [[pysift:sculk_scale|sculk scale]] and 4 [[pysift:charoite|charoite]]. Nine make a block of soulite, which works as a beacon base.

=== item:sonic_staff | Sonic Staff
The Harmonizer's weapon. Hold for 0.6 s, then release a sonic blast: 8 damage, 15 blocks, 3 s cooldown; it chains to 3 enemies (3 damage each) and causes soul deprivation.

**Awakened** (smithing table plus another boss's shard): a wide 12-damage blast with a 2 s cooldown, chaining to 5 enemies (4 each). Resonance adds 3 range and 1 damage per level.

=== item:dash_charm | Dash Charm
The Monarch's weapon. Dash up to 2 times in a row, leaving a decoy for 10 s to draw enemies.

**Awakened**: 3 dashes, hurting enemies along the way. Afterimage makes dashes longer, decoys last longer and the cooldown shorter.

=== item:sculk_hammer | Sculk Hammer
The Sculk Monstrosity's weapon, a mace-type weapon with the mace's falling attack.

- Charge and slam: radius 3–4.5, or 6 when fully charged (2 s).
- Sculk maws grow from the ground and explode 2 s later (radius 2.5, 6 damage, soul deprivation).
- **Awakened**: radius 4–6, or 8 fully charged. Aftershock adds 0.75 blocks and 1 maw per level.

=== item:soul_javelin | Soul Javelin
The hunter's weapon, made from a [[pysift:hunter_spearhead|spearhead]] and 2 sticks. Thrown like a trident for about 5–9 damage. Homecoming makes it fly back.

=== item:sift_beacon_gem | Sift Beacon Gem
6 charoite around the three boss cores. Inlay it into any chestplate at a smithing table (gem, chestplate, charoite): the wearer and **every player within 8 blocks** are immune to soul deprivation, and existing deprivation is cleansed.

=== item:soul_antidote | Soul Antidote
Glass bottle, [[pysift:gloomcap|gloomcap]], [[pysift:soul_berries|soul berries]] and an echo shard. Drinking it (0.8 s) removes soul deprivation and the health lock; your health returns unless it shattered. Stacks to 16.

=== item:soul_essence | Soul Essence
Dropped by most monsters killed by a player. Use it on a block to **cycle its state** (for example how far crimson vein stone has spread, or how hot ember stone is), 1 per use; sneak + use picks which state to change. Also a material for the altar, sigils and machines.

=== item:soul_compass | Soul Compass
Points to the three boss arenas or to ruins; sneak + use to switch target.

=== item:homeward_stone | Homeward Stone
Hold for 3 seconds to return to the Overworld. Soul essence, charoite and an ender pearl make 2.

=== item:mourning_horn | Mourning Horn
Blowing it calms Wardens within 24 blocks for 30 s (1 minute cooldown, 32 uses). Blown at the Ancient City frame with a Singer and a Sift Warden beside you, it starts the finale. Lament adds 4 blocks of range per level.

=== item:rift_seal | Rift Seal
Use it on a portal or rift to close it; rot within 12 blocks starts to heal ("Stitched Shut").

=== item:sift_heart | Sift Heart
A [[pysift:sculk_monstrosity_heart|Sculk Monstrosity Heart]] purified in spirit liquid. Use it on a Mourner or a Warden to cure it at once.

=== item:summoning_sigil | Summoning Sigil
Use it on a [[pysift:sift_altar|Sift Altar]] within 48 blocks of an arena to summon that arena's boss again. 4 soul essence, 4 charoite and 1 siftite ingot.

=== item:sift_altar | Sift Altar
Place it near a boss arena and use a [[pysift:summoning_sigil|Summoning Sigil]] on it to fight the boss again. When placed it tells you which arena it is linked to, or where the nearest one is.

=== item:tuning_fork | Tuning Fork
Tunes note blocks and makes nearby Singers sing together (64 uses). Harmony makes Singers further away answer.

=== item:lullaby_censer | Lullaby Censer
Singers within 16 blocks do not go hollow; Mourners within 8 blocks are cured after about 60 s.

=== item:echo_cradle | Echo Cradle
Charges when a Singer is nearby or when fed echo shards (light 3, 6, 9, 12); when full it makes an Echo Golem.

=== item:soul_crystal_block | Soul Crystal Block
Forms where spirit liquid meets water. Blub food and slabber breeding item; in the Sift, a closed frame of these lit with an echo shard makes a portal home. Singers in the Overworld need one nearby to keep from going hollow.

=== item:spirit_liquid_bucket | Bucket of Spirit Liquid
A bucket of [[page:soul|spirit liquid]].

=== item:aurum_apple | Aurum Apple
8 aurum ingots around an apple. Eating it gives Regeneration II for 5 s, Absorption for 2 minutes and Soul Ward for 1 minute.

=== item:gloomcap_stew | Gloomcap Stew
Restores 7 hunger and gives 45 s of Echo Sight (enemies within 32 blocks glow).

=== item:nuzzle_fluff | Nuzzle Fluff
Nuzzle fur. Four make a white wool, and it brews into a Regeneration potion.
