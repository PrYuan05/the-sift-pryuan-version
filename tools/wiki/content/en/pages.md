=== page:story | Story
## The setting: the Overworld infected the Sift
Most people think sculk grew out of the Deep Dark and slowly spread through the world. It is the other way round: **the Overworld infected the Sift.**

**The Sift** is a sieve between souls, a bright and living world. The glowing [[pysift:spirit_liquid_bucket|spirit liquid]] that runs through it is its blood, and the Sift's creatures live on it.

- **Sculk belongs to the Sift.** Healthy sculk is red ([[pysift:red_sculk|red sculk]]). The blue sculk of the Deep Dark is the same sculk, starving and rotting.
- **The Overworld's air has no souls in it.** A Sift creature that leaves the spirit liquid starves and goes hollow, the way a piglin turns into a zombified piglin in the Overworld.

## The out-of-tune ritual of the Ancient City
The people of the Ancient City tried to open a door to the Sift by singing. They set eight resonant jukeboxes in front of the frame, to play eight notes in order.

- The right order is **1 3 7 6 5 2 4 8**.
- They played **1 3 4 6 5 2 4 8**: **the third disc was wrong**.

The door tore open. Red sculk and one Singer were swept into the Overworld, and the ancients died in front of the door.

- **Reinforced deepslate** cannot be mined because the ancients' bones are built into the frame.
- The Singer trapped in the Overworld slowly went hollow and became the [[mob:mourner|Mourner]]. It still sits there, singing the melody backwards, trying to close the door.
- **Wardens** are hollowed Sift creatures too, kin to the Singers. They live only on the souls they swallowed, and they rage at sound because sound opens the door.

## Rifts and rot
The failed opening left cracks across the world: **wild rifts**. Soulless Overworld air leaks back into the Sift through them and rots the red sculk blue. That is the real infection.

The deepest knot of that rot is the final boss, the [[mob:sculk_monstrosity|Sculk Monstrosity]].

=== page:guide | Getting started
This page follows the order you play in. Every step links to the full numbers.

## 1. Getting ready
1. **Find an Ancient City**: in the middle of the Deep Dark's Ancient City stands a reinforced-deepslate frame. City chests hold ancient pages I–IV.
2. **Collect eight discs**: Pigstep, Relic, Precipice, Creator, 5, Otherside, 13 and Tears. See [[page:portal|The Octave Ritual]] for where each comes from.
3. **Bring a goat horn and echo shards**: the horn calls a Singer, and an [[minecraft:echo_shard|echo shard]] asks it to start the ritual.
4. **Plan for antidotes**: spirit liquid is everywhere in the Sift and touching it causes [[page:soul|soul deprivation]]. The [[pysift:soul_antidote|Soul Antidote]] (glass bottle, gloomcap, soul berries, echo shard) needs Sift ingredients, so on your first trip just stay out of the liquid.

## 2. First visit
- You get the [[pysift:sift_codex|Sift Codex]] on arrival. It fills in as you meet creatures and find pages.
- To go home, hold a [[pysift:homeward_stone|Homeward Stone]] for 3 seconds, or build a closed frame of [[pysift:soul_crystal_block|soul crystal blocks]] and use an echo shard on it.
- The Sift has no day or night, only dusk. **Beds do not work**; use a respawn anchor.

## 3. First materials
| Material | Where | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| [[pysift:siftite_ore|Siftite ore]] | Every biome, Y −40 to 40 | Smelts into [[pysift:siftite_ingot|siftite ingots]]: siftite tools and armor, the altar, gears |
| [[pysift:charoite_cluster|Charoite clusters]] | Violet Slate Abyss surface and caves | [[pysift:charoite|Charoite]]: the beacon, sigils, soulite |
| [[pysift:aurum_ore|Aurum ore]] | Lullaby Hills riverbeds | Aurum tools (fast, easy to enchant), aurum apples |
| [[pysift:patina_ore|Patina ore]] | Soul Caldera rift walls | Soul Power pipes and conduits |
| [[pysift:gloomcap|Gloomcap]], [[pysift:soul_berries|soul berries]] | Cave floors; next to spirit liquid ponds | Antidotes, Soul Ward potions, stews |

**A full siftite set** softens the spirit liquid drain from 20 % of your health to 50 %. Make it first.

## 4. The three guardians
Each boss lives in an arena in its own biome. A [[pysift:soul_compass|Soul Compass]] points to the arenas (sneak + use to switch), or use `/locate boss`.

| Boss | Biome | Core | Shard |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[mob:harmonizer]] | [[biome:willow_grove]] | [[pysift:harmonizer_core]] | [[pysift:resonant_crystal]] |
| [[mob:monarch]] | [[biome:amber_dunes]] | [[pysift:monarch_crown]] | [[pysift:monarch_feather]] |
| [[mob:sculk_monstrosity]] | [[biome:violet_slate_abyss]] | [[pysift:sculk_monstrosity_heart]] | [[pysift:sculk_scale]] |

Each kill drops **one core**, but the weapons and the beacon gem both need cores, so you fight each boss at least twice. To fight again, place a [[pysift:sift_altar|Sift Altar]] within 48 blocks of the arena and use a [[pysift:summoning_sigil|Summoning Sigil]] on it.

## 5. Boss weapons and awakening
| Weapon | Made from | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| [[pysift:sonic_staff]] | Harmonizer core, resonant crystal, siftite ingot | Charge 0.6 s for a sonic blast: 8 damage, 15 blocks, chains to 3 enemies |
| [[pysift:dash_charm]] | Monarch crown, monarch feather, siftite ingot | Up to 2 dashes in a row, leaving a decoy for 10 s |
| [[pysift:sculk_hammer]] | Sculk Monstrosity heart, sculk scale, siftite ingot | Charged slam that grows exploding sculk maws; falling attack like the mace |

At a smithing table, **another boss's shard** awakens a weapon: the staff hits for 12 with a 2 s cooldown and chains to 5; the charm dashes 3 times and hurts enemies on the way; the hammer's radius grows.

## 6. The Sift Beacon
[[pysift:sift_beacon_gem|Sift Beacon Gem]]: 6 charoite and the three cores. Inlay it into any chestplate at a smithing table; the wearer and every player within 8 blocks are **immune to soul deprivation**, and existing deprivation is cleansed.

## 7. Soulite gear
- [[pysift:soulite_ingot|Soulite ingot]]: netherite ingot, resonant crystal, monarch feather, sculk scale and 4 charoite.
- [[pysift:soulite_upgrade_smithing_template|Soulite Upgrade Template]]: 25 % boss drop, also found in chests; duplicate it with charoite.
- At a smithing table: template, netherite gear and a soulite ingot.

Soulite (durability 2600, armor 4/9/7/4, toughness 4) gives every piece its own power, and the full set is immune to soul deprivation. See the [[pysift:soulite_sword|Soulite Sword]] page.

## 8. The ending
Beat the [[mob:sculk_monstrosity|Sculk Monstrosity]] and the rot stops. Stitch the rifts with [[pysift:rift_seal|Rift Seals]], then take a Singer and a Sift Warden back to the Ancient City and blow the [[pysift:mourning_horn|Mourning Horn]] for the [[page:story|finale]].

=== page:portal | The Octave Ritual and Portals
## Opening the door to the Sift
1. Stand within 16 blocks of the Ancient City's **reinforced-deepslate** frame and **blow a goat horn**. If no other Singer is within 24 blocks, a ritual [[mob:singer|Singer]] appears.
2. Use an [[minecraft:echo_shard|echo shard]] on the Singer. Two [[mob:echo_golem|Echo Golems]] set **8 resonant jukeboxes** in front of the frame.
3. Each jukebox shines a coloured beam. Insert the matching disc left to right in **rainbow order**:

| Seat | Disc | Beam | Where to find it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pigstep | Red | Bastion remnants |
| 2 | Relic | Orange | Trail ruins (suspicious gravel) |
| 3 | Precipice | Yellow | Trial chambers |
| 4 | Creator | Green | Ominous vaults |
| 5 | 5 | Blue | Ancient cities (disc fragments) |
| 6 | Otherside | Indigo | Strongholds, ancient cities |
| 7 | 13 | Violet | Dungeons |
| 8 | Tears | Pink | Killing a ghast |

- **All correct**: the frame fills with a portal. Stand in it for 2 seconds to travel.
- **Any wrong disc**: the door tears, dark sculk bursts out and all discs pop out. Just insert them again.
- **The jukeboxes are the key**: break any one and the door closes.

## Getting home
- In the Sift, build a closed frame of [[pysift:soul_crystal_block|soul crystal blocks]] and use an echo shard on it to make a portal home.
- Or hold a [[pysift:homeward_stone|Homeward Stone]] for 3 seconds.

## Wild rifts
About one in 600 chunks of the Sift has a **wild rift**: a small door back to the Overworld ringed with blue-and-red rot. Rifts never appear in the underground layers.

=== page:soul | Soul Deprivation and Spirit Liquid
## Spirit liquid
[[pysift:spirit_liquid_bucket|Spirit liquid]] is the Sift's blood. It flows like water, glows (light 10) and fits in a bucket.

| When | Result |
|---|---|
| A player touches it | **Soul deprivation** (below) |
| Another mob touches it | 3 s of soul deprivation |
| A Warden or Mourner touches it | They lack souls, so they drink it instead of getting hurt (and start to be cured) |
| It meets a lava source | [[pysift:void_obsidian|Void obsidian]] |
| It meets flowing lava | [[pysift:void_stone|Void stone]] |
| It meets water | [[pysift:soul_crystal_block|Soul crystal block]] |
| Waterloggable blocks | Can be "spirit-logged" as well |

## Soul deprivation
- Triggered by touching spirit liquid; lasts 20 seconds.
- **Your health is drained to 20 %** (at least 1 point).
- **Soul shatter**: take any damage while deprived and the drained 80 % breaks; your maximum health stays locked at 20 % until you drink an antidote or die.
- While deprived, **food does not heal you**, and water or rain will not put out the soul flames.
- Your view turns sculk-like, soul flames rise from your body and your hearts become soul-fire hearts.
- On mobs: +30 % damage taken and about 1.25 health lost per second.

| Soul Ward potion | Awkward potion + [[pysift:soul_berries|soul berries]]; immune for 3:00 (8:00 extended) |
| [[pysift:soul_nectar|Soul Nectar]] | 10 s of Soul Ward |
| Soul Ward enchantment | Helmet or chestplate; immunity |
| [[pysift:sift_beacon_gem|Sift Beacon]] | Inlaid in a chestplate: you and players within 8 blocks are immune and cleansed |

=== page:rot | Rot and Sealing
## The rot spreads
Soulless Overworld air flows into the Sift through portals and rifts. [[pysift:red_sculk|Red sculk]] **within 10 blocks of a portal** slowly rots into vanilla blue sculk.

## Stopping the rot
- **Defeat the [[mob:sculk_monstrosity|Sculk Monstrosity]]**: the world records that the rot has stopped, and blue sculk everywhere slowly turns red again.
- **[[pysift:rift_seal|Rift Seal]]**: use it on a portal to close it; rot within 12 blocks starts to heal.
- **[[pysift:soul_sifter|Soul Sifter]]**: a Soul Power machine that turns blue sculk within 8 blocks red.
- **[[mob:sift_warden|Sift Warden]]**: blue sculk it walks over turns red.

=== page:soul_power | Soul Power
The Sift's redstone. In the Sift, a signal is a song; vanilla redstone is "a signal with no soul".

## Signal
- **Strength 0–15**: 15 next to a [[pysift:spirit_engine|Spirit Engine]], dropping by 1 every 2 blocks.
- **SP (soul pressure)**: every running engine adds 256 SP of capacity. Machine speed = 32 × strength ÷ 15.
- **Overload**: when use exceeds capacity the signal drops to 0, and after 5 s every creature within 6 blocks gets soul deprivation.
- A [[pysift:soul_conduit|Soul Conduit]] accepts levers, repeaters, comparators, redstone torches and powered blocks, **but not redstone dust**. A vanilla signal pauses machines and switches engines off.
- Use any block on a conduit to hide the conduit inside that block.
- Wireless signal only goes through [[pysift:charoite_beam_emitter|charoite beams]].

## Special sources
- **Singers**: a [[mob:singer|Singer]] within 3 blocks of a conduit sings into it (strength 15, 64 SP), then rests for 30 s.
- **Sculk catalysts**: a blooming catalyst acts as a source for 10 s (32 SP).

## Kinetic add-on
Registered only when `config/pysift.properties` sets `kinetic_addon=on` (the default, auto, leaves it off). A Create-style rotation system:

- Signed RPM and gear ratios; the engine turns at 32 RPM, the [[pysift:soul_hand_crank|hand crank]] at 32 RPM × 3 SP.
- Conflicting directions or more than 256 RPM jam the network.
- [[pysift:soul_shaft]], [[pysift:soul_cogwheel]], [[pysift:large_soul_cogwheel]], [[pysift:soul_gearbox]], [[pysift:soul_clutch]], [[pysift:soul_gearshift]]: transmission.
- [[pysift:soul_fan]]: pushes items; blowing through soul fire it "haunts" them (sand → soul sand, dirt → soul soil, torch → soul torch, sculk vein → red sculk vein).
- [[pysift:soul_drill]]: breaks the block in front (hardness × 30 ticks).
- A Resonance Array on a kinetic network runs 1.5× faster.

=== page:mobs_intro | (mobs)
The Sift has about thirty creatures: Singers and Echo Golems that sing, the tameable blub and Sift Warden, three bosses, and the monsters of each biome. Twelve of them have [[page:climate|tropical and cold variants]]. Numbers are read straight from the mod (hearts in brackets).

=== page:dimension | (dimension)
The Sift (`pysift:the_sift`) runs from Y −64 to 320 and is held at a mint-coloured dusk.

- **Beds do not work**; respawn anchors do.
- A day has three phases, and biomes are half the vanilla size.
- There are 6 surface biomes; digging down takes you through 4 underground layers chosen by **depth below the surface**.
- Every biome has [[pysift:siftite_ore|siftite ore]] (Y −40 to 40) and soul geodes (one per 18 chunks: a void obsidian shell around soul crystal blocks).
- On the surface (except the volcano) there is a sinkhole about every 14 chunks and a wild rift every 600.

| Layer | Depth | What is there |
|---|---|---|
| [[biome:withered_hollows]] | 26–54 blocks below the surface | Dry cracks, blue sculk, pointed dark slate, gloomcap |
| [[biome:red_zone_caves]] | 54–87 blocks | Red sculk, pointed crimson vein stone, hanging orange sift vines, charoite |
| [[biome:resonant_vaults]] | Below 87 (dry) | Pointed void stone, charoite on the ceiling, soul crystal blocks |
| [[biome:spirit_deeps]] | Below 87 (wet) | Spirit liquid lakes, magma-vein floors, hanging carapace vines, glowshade grass |

=== page:building_sets | Building families
Every Sift stone has a full building set: the stone, bricks and chiseled bricks, each with slabs, stairs and walls. Void stone, void obsidian, soulshale and varied dark slate also have polished, cracked, tile and smooth forms.

- **Bricks**: 4 stone (2×2) → 4 bricks.
- **Chiseled bricks**: 2 brick slabs stacked.
- **Polished**: 4 stone → 4 polished; **tiles**: 4 bricks → 4 tiles.
- **Smooth**: smelt the stone; **cracked**: smelt the bricks.
- **Pillar**: 2 stone stacked → 2 pillars.
- **Stonecutter**: every form cuts into slabs, stairs and walls.
- [[pysift:soulshale|Soulshale]]: 8 abyssal strata around 1 soul sand or soul soil → 8.

=== page:boss_common | Boss rules
All three bosses follow these rules:
- Below 50 % health they enter **phase 2**: +30 % attack, +15 % speed, skill cooldowns × 0.65, and they call reinforcements.
- With no target they heal 2 % of their health per second, and walk back if they get more than 20 blocks from the arena centre.
- Each kill drops: 1 core, 2–4 shards, 3–6 soul essence, 2–4 siftite ingots, and a 25 % chance of the soulite upgrade template.
- **Rematch**: place a [[pysift:sift_altar|Sift Altar]] within 48 blocks of the arena and use a [[pysift:summoning_sigil|Summoning Sigil]] on it.
