=== mob:blub | Blub
The Sift's mascot: a round, bubbling jelly you can tame like a wolf.

## Behaviour
- Eats only [[pysift:soul_crystal_block|soul crystal blocks]]. Holding one lures it; each feeding has a 1 in 3 chance to tame it, and the same block breeds and heals it.
- Once tamed it follows you, can sit, and fights for you (4 attack).
- It carries dropped items within 8 blocks back into its owner's inventory.
- Below half health it plays a sulking animation.
- About 35 % of wild blubs spawn as a **stack of four**. When someone nearby blows a goat horn the whole stack bounces in a wave ("Stack 'Em Up!").

## Five variants
Decided by the biome it is born in:
| Variant | Where |
|---|---|
| Soul-engorged (default) | Soul Caldera, half of the Willow Grove |
| Carapace | Amber Dunes |
| Meadows | The other half of the Willow Grove |
| Ravines | Crimson Strata Canyon, Lullaby Hills |
| Fungal | Violet Slate Abyss, and anywhere below Y 40 |

=== mob:nuzzle | Nuzzle
A fluffy four-legged creature with a mop of fur on its back. The Sift's sheep.

## Behaviour
- Walks up to players within 10 blocks and **nuzzles** them, giving 10 s of Glowing and Regeneration I (60 s cooldown).
- Squeals when hit and runs at 1.5× speed.
- Breeds with [[pysift:soul_berries|soul berries]]; babies have their own look and inherit the fur colour. A baby nuzzling your feet gives 6 s of Regeneration ("Fluffy Warmth").

## Shearing and dyeing
- **Shears** take 1–3 wool: natural fur gives red wool, dyed fur gives that colour. The fur grows back in 2–4 minutes.
- It can be dyed **25 colours**: the 16 vanilla colours and the 9 Sift colours (teal, mint, coral, amber, lavender, rose, soul blue, void, dusk gold).
- Tropical nuzzles keep their hibiscus flowers until sheared; cold ones have frosted fur.

=== mob:slabber | Slabber
A large grazer carrying a slab of earth on its back. The Sift's cow: you cannot milk it, but you can grow flowers on it.

## Planting flowers
- Use any flower on its bare back to plant it.
- It grows a stage about every 2 minutes, three stages in all, from 1 flower to 5; bone meal adds a stage at once.
- Harvest with shears: fully grown gives 2–4 of that flower, otherwise you get the one flower back.
- [[mob:pollinator|Pollinators]] pollinate the flowers on its back.

Breeds with [[pysift:soul_crystal_block|soul crystal blocks]].

=== mob:lootkin | Lootkin
A shy, very fast little creature that carries treasure.

- Runs from any player within 12 blocks. It does not run if you hold a gold ingot, an amethyst shard or a [[pysift:siftite_ingot|siftite ingot]]; those three are also its breeding food.
- Baby lootkin pick up gold nuggets or amethyst from the ground and give you a small Sift trinket in return.
- Generous drops: 2–3 rolls from iron, gold, lapis, emerald, charoite, diamond and siftite ingots, plus an 8 % chance of a bucket of spirit liquid.

=== mob:echo_golem | Echo Golem
A stone giant carrying a [[pysift:soul_crystal_block|soul crystal block]] on its head. Neutral: it only fights back.

## Combat
- Punches; slams the ground when the target is within 2.5 blocks; fires an echo pulse (damage and slowness) at 3.5–12 blocks.

## Souls and crystals
- Gathers souls from soul sand and soul soil, up to 16, and absorbs naturally generated soul crystal blocks, up to 3.
- With no players around it gives the crystal on its head to a [[mob:singer|Singer]]. Use an empty hand on it to take the crystal; a new one forms in about 3 minutes.
- **Contract**: use an [[minecraft:echo_shard|echo shard]] on it (uses 1). Every 30 s it turns 2 souls into 1 soul soil or soul sand for its owner, with a 5 % chance of a bonus echo shard.
- Two adult golems fed soul crystal blocks make a baby, which grows up in about 20 minutes. A fully charged [[pysift:echo_cradle|Echo Cradle]] also makes a golem.
- Half of naturally spawned golems come with a Singer; in the Octave Ritual they are the ones who set out the jukeboxes.

=== mob:singer | Singer
A tall, thin Sift creature that sings: the heart of the story. Passive.

- When someone blows a goat horn within 16 blocks it sings a harmony, giving every player within 16 blocks Resistance I for 30 s (10 s cooldown per Singer).
- It climbs out of the ground when it spawns (3 s).
- Ring a [[pysift:tuning_fork|tuning fork]] and Singers sing together: a lullaby in the low range, Twinkle Twinkle in the middle, Ode to Joy at the top.
- Near a [[pysift:soul_conduit|soul conduit]] it sings into it and becomes a Soul Power source.

## Story
- When it sees a Warden within 24 blocks it trembles, then sings the octave backwards and the Warden calms down for 30 s.
- In the Overworld, with no soul crystal block or spirit liquid within 6 blocks, it **goes hollow and becomes a [[mob:mourner|Mourner]]** after about 5 minutes. A [[pysift:lullaby_censer|Lullaby Censer]] prevents this within 16 blocks.
- The Octave Ritual and the finale both need it; see [[page:story|Story]].

=== mob:mourner | Mourner
A hollowed Singer. It still sits in front of the Ancient City's door, singing the opening melody backwards.

- Neutral; runs away when hit.
- When a player comes within 6 blocks it stops and weeps the octave backwards, blinding players within 12 blocks with Darkness (30 s cooldown).
- In the Overworld it spawns only in the **Deep Dark** (rarely); it also turns up in the Sift's Withered Hollows.
- **Bring it back**: 10 s in spirit liquid, about a minute within 8 blocks of a [[pysift:lullaby_censer|Lullaby Censer]], or a [[pysift:sift_heart|Sift Heart]]. It turns back into a Singer ("Homeward Song").

=== mob:sift_warden | Sift Warden
A Warden that has been cured. It has finally drunk spirit liquid and no longer lives on swallowed souls.

## How to get one
- Let a Warden soak in spirit liquid for 15 s, take it to the Sift for 30 s, or use a [[pysift:sift_heart|Sift Heart]] on it.
- Sift Wardens also stand guard in the Echo Den.

## Once tamed
- Feed it 1 [[minecraft:echo_shard|echo shard]] to tame it; it follows you, use it to make it sit, and feed it sculk to heal it.
- 15 melee damage (half a Warden's), and its sonic boom is halved too.
- While following you, monsters within 16 blocks glow every 3 s, even through walls.
- It only attacks monsters that have fought its owner, and keeps wild Wardens within 24 blocks calm.
- Blue sculk it walks over turns red again.

=== mob:pollinator | Pollinator
A big bee that walks instead of flying. Neutral.

- Applies a bone meal effect to crops or saplings within 8 blocks, up to 3 times (20 s cooldown), and pollinates the flowers on [[mob:slabber|slabbers]].
- Hit one and every pollinator within 8 blocks turns on you; stings poison for 5 s.
- Drops [[pysift:soul_nectar|Soul Nectar]] (drink for Soul Ward) and honeycomb.

=== mob:dartback | Dartback
A flat reptile covered in spines, the most common monster of the red canyons.

- Keeps 6–12 blocks away and fires a spine dart every 2 s (3 damage).
- At 4–10 blocks it paws the ground for 0.75 s and charges in a straight line: 7 damage and knockback, 7 s cooldown. Step aside when you see it paw.
- **Hits from behind do half damage**; attack from the front or side.
- Its [[pysift:dartback_spine|spines]] make [[pysift:spine_arrow|spine arrows]].

=== mob:fusefly | Fusefly
A flying bug with a fuse on its head.

- When it dies it swells and explodes about 1 s later: 4-block radius, 12 magic damage, **no block damage**.
- The flashing warning ring shows the blast radius. Shoot it from a distance.
- Drops [[pysift:fuse_powder|fuse powder]], which works in place of gunpowder.

=== mob:hurler | Hurler
A monster that digs up rocks and throws them.

- At 3–20 blocks it throws rubble: 2-block landing radius, 6 damage, 4 s cooldown. Up close it uses melee.
- Drops [[pysift:hurler_rock|hurler rocks]] (throwable, 4 damage).

=== mob:prickle | Prickle
A small monster covered in quills.

- When hit in melee it **reflects 30 % of the damage** and fires a ring of 8 quills. Bows and long weapons are safer.
- Drops [[pysift:prickle_quill|prickle quills]] for spine arrows.

=== mob:wobble | Wobble
A wobbling jelly monster.

- On death it leaves a nauseating mist: 3-block radius for 3 s; anyone inside gets 8 s of Nausea.
- Drops [[pysift:wobble_jelly|wobble jelly]] (30 % chance of Nausea when eaten).

=== mob:nester | Nester
A large mother creature that hatches a swarm.

- In combat it calls 2 [[mob:seedling|seedlings]] or [[mob:sprout|sprouts]] every 8 s.
- When it dies, young within 16 blocks go berserk: Speed II and Strength I for 30 s. Clear the young before the mother.
- Drops [[pysift:nester_egg|nester eggs]] (fry them to eat) and feathers.

=== mob:seedling | Seedling
A swarm young of the nester.

- Once one targets you, every seedling within 12 blocks joins in.
- **Takes 3× fire and fall damage**: fire, lava or a drop works well.

=== mob:sprout | Sprout
The nester's other young; behaves like the [[mob:seedling|seedling]]: swarms, and hates fire and falls.

=== mob:stalker | Stalker
A hunter that lurks in the dark.

- **Invisible below light level 7**; torches or an Echo Sight potion help.
- Circles behind its target; its first hit deals 200 %. It backs off about 5 blocks when hit.
- Drops [[pysift:shadow_hide|shadow hide]] for invisibility potions.

=== mob:scavenger | Scavenger
A hooded carrion eater with curved horns.

- Goes for prey **below half health** first.
- Its bite makes the target bleed for 5 s (1 per second) until the scavenger dies.
- Drops [[pysift:scavenger_fang|scavenger fangs]] (for [[pysift:bleeding_arrow|bleeding arrows]]) and gold nuggets.

=== mob:hunter | Hunter
One of the Monarch's soldiers, a javelin thrower.

- Keeps 8–14 blocks away and throws [[pysift:soul_javelin|soul javelins]] at 5–18 blocks (about 6 damage).
- **Mark**: makes the target glow for 8 s, and every Sift monster within 24 blocks switches to it (15 s cooldown).
- Leaps back when you close in.
- Drops [[pysift:hunter_spearhead|spearheads]]; add two sticks for a soul javelin.

=== mob:trapper | Trapper
One of the Monarch's soldiers, who sets traps.

- Buries soul snares in your path: 3 damage and 2.5 s rooted, up to 3 at once.
- Throws nets: Slowness III for 4 s and 1 s rooted.
- Drops [[pysift:trapper_net|trapper nets]] (throw them to snare enemies).

=== mob:sentinel | Sentinel
A heavily armoured warrior in a carapace shell.

- Heavy cleave: 0.8 s wind-up, 12 damage; if you are blocking, your shield breaks and goes on a 3 s cooldown. Back off when it winds up.
- Drops [[pysift:raw_siftite|raw siftite]].

=== mob:tuner | Tuner
A support monster that never attacks.

- Stays at least 6 blocks from players and pulses an aura every 3 s, giving monsters within 12 blocks Speed I and Strength I for 4 s. **Take it out first.**
- Drops amethyst shards and, rarely, a [[pysift:tuning_fork|tuning fork]].

=== mob:bloombud | Bloombud
A monster disguised as a closed flower bud. It never moves.

- When a player comes within 2 blocks it snaps shut: 6 damage and Slowness IV for 3 s.
- Drops [[pysift:soul_berries|soul berries]] and [[pysift:bloombud_petal|bloombud petals]] (yellow dye).

=== mob:roamroot | Roamroot
A walking root giant, the big one of the Willow Grove.

- Never starts a fight; only fights back.
- Stomp: 3.5-block radius, 7 damage and knockback, 4 s cooldown.
- Drops [[pysift:roamroot_tuber|roamroot tubers]] (roast them to eat) and soul willow saplings.

=== mob:harmonizer | Harmonizer
Guardian of the Willow Grove, a giant jellyfish. One of the three guardians in the codex.

## Combat
- Keeps 11–17 blocks from its target.
- **Sonic cone**: 60° fan, 8 damage, ignores armor.
- **Resonance ring**: expands from 2 to 7 blocks, 6 damage; jump over it or stay inside it.
- Slams for 10 when you get close.
- **Roar**: calls 5 monsters and beams Strength and Resistance onto them.
- Tentacle grab for 7; a **purple vortex** pulls you in and slams you for 16 (keep jumping to break free).
- On death it sends nearby monsters berserk; in phase 2 it calls 3 fuseflies.

## Arena
Resonant Spirit Lake Pavilion: a 3-deep ring of spirit liquid, concentric stone rings, 4 bridges, 16 organ-pipe pillars and curtains of willow leaves.

=== mob:monarch | Monarch
Guardian of the Amber Dunes, a cloaked king with a three-pointed crown.

## Combat
- Two-hit and three-hit combos and a spinning kick for 9; 3 % melee lifesteal.
- **Leap slam** for 14; **ground shockwave** for 10 (dodge sideways).
- **Dash spin-slash** for 14 and **circling spin** for 10: both leave feathers that fire at you 5 s later.
- Calls soldiers: stalkers and scavengers above half health, hunters and trappers below.
- Below 3/4 health it summons a translucent clone (30 % health).
- In phase 2 it calls 2 sentinels.

## Arena
Imperial Sunken Ziggurat: a three-tier sunken amphitheatre, a cross of avenues, a central throne altar and 4 obelisks, floored with faded sandstone bricks.

=== mob:sculk_monstrosity | Sculk Monstrosity
Guardian of the Violet Slate Abyss and the deepest knot of the rot. The final boss.

## Combat
- Left-right combos; a **twisting sweep** of 200° for 12.
- **Sculk barrage**: 5 rolling blocks that chase you, 7 each.
- **Summons sculk catalysts**: 3 of them burst after 6 s unless mined (10 damage), and each one heals it 5 %. **Mine the catalysts first.**
- Slam for 12; sonic blast for 10, ignoring armor.
- **Soul-fire charge** for 18, followed by a punch for 16 that sets you on fire.
- In phase 2 it gives you Darkness every 5 s and calls 2 stalkers.

## After the fight
- The rot stops everywhere and blue sculk slowly turns red again ("The Rot Recedes").
- It carries ancient page VIII.
- Drop the [[pysift:sculk_monstrosity_heart|Sculk Monstrosity Heart]] into spirit liquid to purify it into a [[pysift:sift_heart|Sift Heart]].

## Arena
Abyssal Titan Crucible: a sunken ravine, 4 ten-block-tall bastions, soul crystal conduits and a cracked seal in the middle.
