=== structure:willow_hut | Willow Stilt Hut
A small hut of [[pysift:soul_willow_planks|soul willow]] in the Willow Grove (9×11×11), raised on 3-block stilts under a slate roof, with a chest and lanterns inside.

- Besides general supplies the chest holds soul willow saplings and planks.

=== structure:dune_tomb | Dune Tomb
A sandstone-brick burial chamber half sunk into the Amber Dunes (11×8×11, 5 blocks below the surface). A ladder leads down through an opening in the roof to a sarcophagus and 2 chests.

- The chests are richer in gold ingots and emeralds.

=== structure:humbler_husk | Humbler Husk
The skeleton of a giant beast half buried in the Amber Dunes (23×20×46). You can walk in between the ribs; there are chests inside the ribcage and the skull.

- Walking inside grants the advancement "Carapace Explorer".
- The chests hold bone blocks, bones, gold, emeralds and fuse powder.

=== structure:strata_shrine | Strata Shrine
A cracked platform of crimson bricks in the Crimson Strata Canyon (11×8×11), ringed by 8 broken pillars, with a chest on the central altar and ember braziers beside it.

- The chest holds general supplies, fuse powder and ember stone; its rare slots include siftite ingots, Soul Antidotes, echo shards, Summoning Sigils, diamonds, spirit liquid buckets and the soulite upgrade template.

=== structure:abyss_obelisk | Abyss Obelisk
A deepslate obelisk in the Violet Slate Abyss (9×13×9), crowned with [[pysift:soul_crystal_block|soul crystal blocks]], with a chest at its foot.

=== structure:echo_den | Echo Den
The Singers' choir sanctuary, a large cave structure deep underground. Echo Golems are born here and healthy Sift Wardens stand guard. It can appear under every biome (spacing 40 chunks).

## The central hall
- A round amphitheatre with a crystal stage in the middle. A choir of 3 Singers stands on the stage, one of them the **lead**: with a player within 24 blocks it sings the right octave every 20 s, and an angry guard that hears it calms down.
- 4 Echo Golems sit in the front row, and a Sift Warden guards the north door.

## Side rooms
| Room | What is there |
|---|---|
| Corridors, crossings, stairs | Connect the rooms |
| Crystal cave | Where young golems are born; 1 Singer and an [[pysift:echo_cradle|Echo Cradle]] |
| Resonance room | The colours of its 8 candles give the order of the ritual discs; 1 Singer |
| Collapsed room | A caved-in room |
| Vault | Guarded by 1 Warden; the chest holds echo shards, diamonds, Summoning Sigils and always one treasure enchanted book |
| Power room | A Singer singing into a soul conduit |

## Guard rules
The guards wake up and turn on a player who:
- attacks a Singer or an Echo Golem;
- opens a chest;
- breaks a crystal cluster, soul crystal block, charoite or note block.

Guards calm down twice as fast while a Singer nearby is singing.

=== structure:arena_harmonizer | Resonant Spirit Lake Pavilion
The [[mob:harmonizer|Harmonizer]]'s arena on the surface of the [[biome:willow_grove|Willow Grove]] (47×30×47).

- A 3-deep lake of spirit liquid surrounds it, crossed by 4 bridges.
- In the middle are concentric stone rings, 16 organ-pipe pillars and curtains of willow leaves; the floor is abyssal strata bricks.
- About 1 in 900 chunks; the boss is already there and never despawns. Find it with a [[pysift:soul_compass|Soul Compass]] or `/locate boss harmonizer`.

=== structure:arena_monarch | Imperial Sunken Ziggurat
The [[mob:monarch|Monarch]]'s arena on the surface of the [[biome:amber_dunes|Amber Dunes]] (47×30×47).

- A three-tier sunken amphitheatre, with a cross of avenues leading to the throne altar and an obelisk at each corner; the floor is faded sandstone bricks.
- Find it with `/locate boss monarch`.

=== structure:arena_sculk_monstrosity | Abyssal Titan Crucible
The [[mob:sculk_monstrosity|Sculk Monstrosity]]'s arena in the [[biome:violet_slate_abyss|Violet Slate Abyss]] (47×30×47).

- A sunken ravine, 4 ten-block-tall bastions, conduits of soul crystal blocks and a cracked seal in the middle; the floor is deepslate bricks.
- Find it with `/locate boss sculk_monstrosity`.

=== structure:lullaby_shrine | Lullaby Shrine
A round stone platform built only on hilltops (15×9×15): 6 pillars around a central jukebox and a ring of note blocks, where 2 Echo Golems hum lullabies. No chest.

- On hilltops in the Crimson Strata Canyon, Violet Slate Abyss, Willow Grove and Lullaby Hills, about 1 in 8 chunks.

=== structure:strata_spire | Strata Spire
A flat-topped mesa of stacked 2-block-high discs (radius 8–18) with stepped edges, colour bands on the sides and vines hanging off the cliffs. About 1 in 14 chunks across 5 surface biomes.

=== structure:sinkhole | Sinkhole
A funnel that drops all the way to a chamber at the depth of the Withered Hollows; about a third have a thin fall of spirit liquid. About one every 14 chunks in the surface biomes except the volcano.

=== structure:wild_rift | Wild Rift
A crack left behind by the failed opening: a small door back to the Overworld, ringed with blue-and-red rot. About one in 600 chunks, never underground. Close it with a [[pysift:rift_seal|Rift Seal]]; see [[page:rot|Rot and Sealing]].

=== structure:fossils | Surface fossils
Only in the [[biome:amber_dunes|Amber Dunes]], about one every 5 chunks, in 4 shapes: rib arch, spine, skull and claw.

=== biome:willow_grove | Willow Grove
The only Sift biome with rain. Giant [[pysift:soul_willow_log|soul willows]] trail glowing leaves over red-capped strata, and it has the most spirit liquid ponds. The [[mob:harmonizer|Harmonizer]]'s arena is here.

=== biome:amber_dunes | Amber Dunes
Rolling amber dunes over bleached and cracked sandstone, scattered with giant fossils and the Humbler Husk. The [[mob:monarch|Monarch]]'s arena is here.

=== biome:violet_slate_abyss | Violet Slate Abyss
A plateau of violet-capped dark slate. Charoite clusters grow from the ground and the caves, and soul crystal blocks lie on the surface and below. The [[mob:sculk_monstrosity|Sculk Monstrosity]]'s arena is here.

=== biome:crimson_strata_canyon | Crimson Strata Canyon
Buttes and canyons of crimson and silted strata with ember stone walls. Home of the Strata Shrine and of the dartbacks.

=== biome:lullaby_hills | Lullaby Hills
Void-black hilltops and crimson vein stone walls. The canyon floors carry [[pysift:aurum_ore|aurum ore]] along the riverbeds, fading from rich ore to bleached sandstone and ash marble roads. Lullaby Shrines sit on the hilltops.

=== biome:soul_caldera | Soul Caldera
A rusted blackstone volcano cone over plains of ember basalt, with spirit liquid running through magma-vein rifts and a crater lake at Y 115. [[pysift:patina_ore|Patina ore]] grows in the rift walls.

=== biome:withered_hollows | Withered Hollows
The shallowest underground layer: dry cracks cut off from the spirit liquid, with blue sculk, pointed dark slate and gloomcap. Sometimes a Mourner sits here weeping.

=== biome:red_zone_caves | Red Zone Caves
Broad winding tunnels cut by water, with rose-grey floors, red sculk, pointed crimson vein stone and hanging orange sift vines.

=== biome:resonant_vaults | Resonant Vaults
Dry domed halls at the bottom of the world, the ceiling thick with charoite and the floor set with pointed void stone and soul crystal blocks.

=== biome:spirit_deeps | Spirit Deeps
Wet caves at the bottom of the world, where spirit liquid is born: lakes of spirit liquid, magma-vein floors, hanging carapace vines and glowing glowshade grass.
