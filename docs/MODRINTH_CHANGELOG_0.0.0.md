## v0.0.0 — Initial Release (The Sift — PrYuan's Version)

An unofficial fan-made Fabric mod for **Minecraft 26.3**, **inspired by *Minecraft Dungeons 2***, introducing **The Sift** dimension with custom-shaded textures, climate variants, bosses, enchantments, and synthesized vocals.

### Highlights
- **10 Handcrafted Biomes & Landmarks:** 6 surface biomes (*Willow Grove*, *Crimson Strata Canyon*, *Violet Slate Abyss*, *Amber Dunes*, *Soul Caldera*, *Lullaby Hills*) + 4 underground layers (*Red Zone Caves*, *Withered Hollows*, *Resonant Vaults*, *Spirit Deeps*), plus *The Echo Den*, *Humbler Husks*, *Lullaby Hilltop Shrines*, biome ruins, and underground Soul Crystal geodes.
- **33+ Animated Creatures & 3 Bosses:**
  - **Custom-Shaded Mob Textures (33 Base Mobs):** All base mob textures are custom-shaded with a 6-step pixel-art pipeline (`1:1` UV) rather than reusing vanilla or spin-off textures.
  - **12 Climate-Variant Mobs × 3 Climates (36 Variants):** Normal, Tropical, and Cold models & textures spawning naturally across all 3 climates exclusively within The Sift.
  - **12 Emissive Glowmasks** for 10 naturally luminescent creatures, plus custom **Sculk Maw** & **Sculk Monstrosity** shading.
  - **3 Multi-Phase Bosses:** *Harmonizer*, *Monarch*, and *Sculk Monstrosity*.
- **Nuzzle Shearing & 25-Color Climate-Adapted Dyeing:** Dyeable with all **16 vanilla dyes + 9 custom Sift dyes** (*Teal, Mint, Coral, Amber, Lavender, Rose, Soul Blue, Void, Dusk Gold*), dropping matching wool blocks when sheared and adapting visually to Normal, Tropical (blooming flowers), and Cold (frosty winter fleece) climates.
- **Singers, Mourners & Custom Synthesized Vocals:** Interactive 8-disc Ancient City portal ritual, *Tuning Fork* choir harmonies, *Mourning Horn*, *Lullaby Censer*, and 49 custom DSP-synthesized multi-register vocal files (`B3 / F#4 / C#5`) with zero copyrighted recordings.
- **10 Sift Enchantments & Combat Feel:** 4 everyday enchantments (*Hemorrhage*, *Ensnare*, *Rift Step*, *Soul Harvest*) + 6 structure treasures (*Soul Rend*, *Soul Ward*, *Afterimage*, *Harmony*, *Lament*, *Homecoming*), Soulite gear progression, Soul Power machines, 450+ blocks, and in-hand weapon physics compatible with *Punchy!* / *Punchy! Moves*.
